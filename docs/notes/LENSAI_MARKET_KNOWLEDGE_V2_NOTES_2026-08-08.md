# LensAI Indonesia Capital Market Knowledge v2

Patch ini hanya memperluas knowledge layer LensAI yang sudah digunakan oleh `/api/chat`.

Cakupan baru antara lain:
- Free float dan ownership structure
- Market cap vs free-float-adjusted market cap
- Likuiditas, spread, depth, ADV/ADTV, turnover, slippage
- ARA/ARB, suspensi, UMA
- Corporate actions
- Fundamental, valuation, technical
- Risk management & backtest interpretation
- Foreign flow dan broker activity
- Indeks, sector heatmap, breadth, regime
- Dividen, rights issue, IPO
- Financial sector / bank awareness
- Cyclicals/commodities
- Macro Indonesia
- Guardrail anti-halusinasi untuk angka live, regulasi terkini, dan formula SahamLens

Tidak mengubah scoring, TP/CL, universe, database, atau UI.
